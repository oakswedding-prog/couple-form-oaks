RIYA JACOB & ROSHAN MATHEW
THE COUPLE STORY & WEDDING BRIEF

==================================================
01 — THEIR STORY
==================================================

Riya and Roshan have been together for almost two years. Their story took a beautiful turn when Roshan secretly planned a surprise trip to Switzerland without revealing the destination to Riya. At a breathtaking mountain location, surrounded by nature, he got down on one knee and proposed. What followed was an unforgettable moment filled with love, surprise and a kiss that made everything official.

Two months later, they introduced each other to their families. Both families were overjoyed, perhaps even more excited about the wedding than the couple themselves!

==================================================
02 — THE LITTLE THINGS THEY LOVE
==================================================

They enjoy travelling, exploring food and cafés, watching movies and series, spending time in nature, and listening to music and attending concerts. They appreciate experiences, little adventures and simply enjoying each other's company.

==================================================
03 — THEIR FAMILY MATTERS
==================================================

Family is an important part of their story. The people they particularly want us to look out for are:

- Mini — Mother (Riya's side)
- Francis — Father (Riya's side)
- Gladson — Sibling
- Michelle — Cousin

Their grandparents are especially important to them. Their blessings, expressions, emotional reactions and little interactions deserve special attention. They want photographs that preserve the love and connections between generations.

==================================================
04 — THEIR VISUAL LANGUAGE
==================================================

They want their wedding to feel natural and candid, elegant and editorial, yet fun and energetic.

Their vision is a combination of timeless cinematic storytelling and fashion-editorial elegance, with genuine emotions and spontaneous interactions at its heart.

They have also chosen two defining wedding tones:

- Fashion-Forward & Editorial
- Traditional & Grand

The goal is to balance sophisticated portraits and beautiful compositions with the energy, traditions and emotions of a grand wedding celebration.

==================================================
05 — MOMENTS THEY ARE LOOKING FORWARD TO
==================================================

The moments they particularly want documented include:

- Getting ready
- First look / first glance
- Ceremony and vows
- Family blessings
- Couple portraits
- Grand entry
- Speeches and toasts

Every moment should be approached with care, from the anticipation before the ceremony to the emotions shared during family blessings and speeches.

==================================================
06 — THEIR COMFORT IN FRONT OF THE CAMERA
==================================================

Both Riya and Roshan expect to be equally emotional during the wedding. They are pretty comfortable in front of the camera and are happy with gentle guidance during portraits and filming.

A few practice shots before the main photographs and seeing previews as the day progresses will help them feel even more comfortable.

==================================================
07 — WHAT MATTERS WHEN CHOOSING THEIR TEAM
==================================================

Their three biggest priorities are:

- Storytelling and film quality
- Personality and how comfortable they feel
- Great communication

They value a team that understands their personalities, communicates well and can translate their story into something meaningful.

==================================================
08 — WHAT THEY WANT TO REMEMBER
==================================================

More than perfectly posed photographs, they want to remember how everything felt.

The laughter, the tears, their parents' expressions, their grandparents' blessings, the excitement of the celebrations and the little moments they might otherwise miss.

When they watch their wedding film years from now, it should take them straight back to that day — allowing them to relive the emotions, hear the voices of the people they love and feel the same excitement all over again.

==================================================
THE FINAL THOUGHT
==================================================

For Riya and Roshan, this isn't just about documenting a wedding. It's about preserving the beginning of a new chapter, surrounded by the people who made their journey special.

Let's give them something that feels alive, deeply personal and timeless — photographs and films that become even more valuable with every passing year.
